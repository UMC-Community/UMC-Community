# UMC-Community

**Universal Minecraft Core (UMC)**

Building an open, collaborative community for technology, development, and shared creativity.

## About Us

UMC-Community is an independent community initiative focused on collaboration, open development, knowledge sharing, and community-driven projects.

We aim to create an inclusive environment where people can explore ideas, share knowledge, contribute to projects, and build things together.

## Our Goals

- **Open Collaboration** — Encourage people to collaborate on projects and ideas.
- **Knowledge Sharing** — Share resources, documentation, and technical knowledge.
- **Community Projects** — Build and maintain useful projects together.
- **Inclusive Participation** — Welcome people with different backgrounds, skills, and experience.

## Get Involved

UMC is at an early stage of development. We welcome people who are interested in our goals and want to participate, contribute, or help shape the community.

More information about participation and community activities will be published as the community grows.
